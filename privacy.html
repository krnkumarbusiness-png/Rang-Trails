Privacy Policy for Rang Trails

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Welcome to Rang Trails. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Rang Trails does not require any account creation or registration. We do not personally collect, store, or sell any personal data such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

2. Local Game Progress

All your puzzle progress, completed levels, unlocked skins, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device secure storage. We do not transmit this progress to any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app integrates third-party services to deliver advertisements (such as banner ads and rewarded video ads for puzzle hints):

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and device telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

Rang Trails requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to load ads (Google AdMob) and verify network connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback during gameplay interactions.

5. Children's Privacy

Rang Trails does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
